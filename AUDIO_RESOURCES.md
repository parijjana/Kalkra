## Technical Note: Asset Pipeline
1.  Download the selected files.
2.  Place SFX in `kalkra/assets/audio/`.
3.  Place BGM in `kalkra/assets/audio/bgm/`.
4.  Standardize extensions to `.ogg` for optimal compression and compatibility.
